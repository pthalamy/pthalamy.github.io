Off the clock

Engineer by day. The rest of the time, mostly this.

When the laptop closes you will usually find me with a guitar in my hands or something slowly coming together on the stove.

I came to music late and on purpose, and it has quietly become what I care about most outside work: fingerstyle guitar above all, a bit of piano to understand what I am hearing, and more singing than I would have admitted a few years ago.

This is the warmer half of the site. Nothing here is on my CV.

A few things

  • Nobody in my family played an instrument. I picked up a roommate's ukulele in my twenties and never quite put it down.
  • One guitar, four voices at once: bass, rhythm, harmony and melody. That is why fingerstyle won.
  • I can no longer hear a doorbell without naming the interval.
  • Cooking is how I show affection. Fermentation is how I practise patience.
  • Grew up where Comté is made. I consider cheese a cultural heritage and act accordingly.

Currently

  • PlayingFingerstyle arrangements, and learning to sing while the right hand keeps going.
  • LearningPiano, mostly as a window into harmony. Ear training most days, usually on a walk.
  • CookingWhatever is in season. There is probably something fermenting in a jar right now.
  • NextPlaying with other people, and finishing a first song I can call my own.
  • On repeatAll the Pretty Girls, by KALEO. Still, after all these years.
  • Fingerpicking heroPassenger, whose arrangements made me want to learn this in the first place.
